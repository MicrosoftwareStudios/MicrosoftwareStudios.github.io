WebSurf


Thank you for using Microsoftware WebSurf, the modern web browser for everyone. Here are a few tips:

• Enter the URL (address) of the website you want to visit in the URL bar at the top of this window.

• Go to the About menu and click on Help (or press F1) to access help.

• Do not try to search in the address bar. Instead, visit a site like Microsoft Bing.

• When the green progress bar shows at the bottom of this window, it means that the browser is busy.
