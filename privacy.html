Our Privacy Policy


We only collect & store data such as your email, phone number (for security & verification), limited device information* & product keys. None of this information is used to send advertising materials or sold to external companies. Any information we collect will not be used for anything other than that for which it was intended to be submitted for by the user.


Software Data Collection

Our software does not collect any personal data from your computer. Only information that is entered by the user will be sent to Microsoftware*. This includes product keys or Microsoftware Account login information. (Note: Signing in with a Microsoftware Account to activate software constitutes entering a product key, therefore product keys collected by during license checks on systems which are signed into a Microsoftware Account falls under this statement.)


* If you activate a product by signing into your Microsoftware Account, your account ID, the license key linked to your account, the product you are activating, and a non-human-readable unique device identifier based on certain physical parts in your computer (such as your CPU) are transmitted to Microsoftware servers for license verification purposes.


COPPA Compliance

Microsoftware cares about kids' online privacy & safety. We will never intentionally collect data from children under the age of 13 without parental consent. Please do not hesitate to contact us if you would like to have your child's information which may have been inadvertently collected without our knowledge removed.
