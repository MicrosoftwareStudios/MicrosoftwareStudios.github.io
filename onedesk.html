Which edition of OneDesk 2025 is right for me?

Whether you're a student, working from home, or the CEO of your own company, OneDesk has the features you need. With three distinct editions, it's never been easier to choose the appropriate product for your specific needs:

  • OneDesk Starter Edition is best for students or those on a low budget, with core features and at a lower price.
  • OneDesk Home Office Edition is great for families and those who work from home. It includes the Skycloud Sync Center and all core apps, as well as Organize and Orbit.
  • OneDesk Pro is the ultimate OneDesk experience, with all the great features, including ChatNet, more SkySync storage, and more productivity power.
